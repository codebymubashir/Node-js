Made slowly, worn for years.

We are a family of weavers, tailors and designers who believe good clothes should outlast the season.

It started with one loom and a grandmother's sewing box.

In 2016, our founder Zarmeen set up a single handloom in her family home in Bahawalpur. She wanted to bring back the zari work her grandmother used to do, which was disappearing from everyday clothing.

Ten years later, we are a team of 180 people. We still weave by hand, we still cut every garment to order, and we still answer our own emails.

What we stand for

Three promises we keep with every order.

✦

Real craft

Our zari is pulled through the fabric by hand. No prints, no shortcuts, and no two pieces are exactly alike.

♻

Made to last

We use natural fabrics and strong stitching, and we offer free repairs for the first year so your clothes stay in your wardrobe, not in landfill.

❤

Fair pay

Every weaver and tailor earns above the local wage, with health cover for their families. You can ask us for the details any time.

Our journey so far

From one loom to a full atelier.

2016

The first loom

Zarmeen weaves her first zari dupatta at home and sells 20 pieces to family and friends.

2019

Our first atelier

We move into a workshop in Bahawalpur with 25 weavers and open our first made-to-measure service.

2022

Menswear arrives

After many requests, we launch our first sherwani and kurta collection for men.

2026

180 artisans, 24 cities

Today we deliver across Pakistan, and every order is still checked by hand before it leaves the atelier.
