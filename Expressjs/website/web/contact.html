Quick answers

Tap a question to open the answer.

How long does delivery take?

Ready-to-wear pieces arrive in 2 to 4 working days. Made-to-measure pieces take about 9 days, because each one is woven and stitched for you.

Can I exchange or return an item?

Yes. You can exchange any unworn piece within 14 days of delivery. Made-to-measure items can be altered for free, but they cannot be returned.

How do I know my size?

Send us your height, chest and waist measurements through the form above, and our tailors will recommend a size or arrange a made-to-measure fitting.

Do you deliver outside Pakistan?

Not yet. We currently deliver to 24 cities across Pakistan, and we plan to add international shipping soon.

How should I wash zari clothing?

Dry clean silk and velvet pieces. Cotton and linen can be hand washed in cold water, turned inside out, and dried in the shade.
