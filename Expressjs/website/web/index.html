How we make it

One garment takes nine days to weave and stitch.

Our weavers pull real zari thread through each fabric by hand. Then master tailors cut it to your measurements, so the piece fits the first time you wear it.

  • Natural fabrics: silk, cotton, linen and wool
  • Free alterations within 14 days of delivery
  • Every artisan is paid above the local wage
